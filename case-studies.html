Enterprise Case Studies & System Architecture

Scrubbed and generalized enterprise solutions demonstrating high-availability automation, financial reconciliation pipelines, and agile governance workflows.

Institutional Cross-Border FX Pricing & Spread Automation Engine

Tech Stack: Google Apps Script, Advanced Spreadsheet Architecture, Dynamic FILTER/INDIRECT Lookups, ISO Currency Standardization.

The Challenge: Decentralized manual rate lookups across branch desks created execution latency, rate slippage, and significant regulatory audit exposure under fair-lending and trade governance mandates.

The Solution: Engineered a unified institutional calculation engine supporting multi-tier volume lookups (Consumer vs. Corporate tiers), automated Quote Direction handling (Multiply/Divide ISO conventions), and real-time gross profit recognition before wire confirmation.

Impact: Eliminated quote latency, guaranteed 0% quotation direction errors, and enforced strict programmatic margin compliance.

Multi-Currency Clearing & Treasury Liquidity Reconciliation System

Tech Stack: Google Apps Script, JavaScript, REST API Integration, Automated PDF Generation, Custom UI Modals.

The Challenge: Daily reconciliation of cross-border wire feeds against correspondent bank statements required manual data exports, prone to boundary corruption, row displacement, and lagging general ledger postings.

The Solution: Developed an automated reconciliation engine featuring boundary-shielded row ingestion, multi-key auto-sorting (separating inflows from outflows and ordering by USD volume), custom UI exception dialogs, and a month-end balance rollover routine that automatically logs year-to-date metrics and clones sub-ledger environments.

Impact: Automated daily trade settlement checks, eliminated manual transcription errors, and produced tamper-evident compliance audit trails.

Enterprise Defect Governance & Live Formula Sync Hub

Tech Stack: JavaScript (Google Apps Script), HTML5/CSS3 Modal Forms, Bidirectional Cell Referencing, Dynamic UX Physics Engine.

The Challenge: Engineering and quality assurance teams suffered from fragmented defect logs, broken hyperlinks when rows were sorted or inserted, and manual overhead copying status updates across tracking sheets.

The Solution: Architected a centralized tracking hub powered by HTML5 custom modals that provision isolated, standardized ticket tabs from a master template. Built a 14-column live formula bus for real-time state synchronization, an automated rich-text archival engine for closed tickets, and dynamic row-height expansion physics for complex multi-paragraph work logs.

Impact: Reduced case setup time by 95% (from minutes to under 2 seconds) and achieved 100% elimination of broken reference errors during release audits.
